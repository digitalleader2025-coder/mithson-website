============================================================

HEADING
- MITHSON HYDRAULIC SEALS

INTRODUCTION
- Hydraulic-cylinder systems operate in extreme conditions.
- This requires high-performance hydraulic seals.
- Mithson describes polymer designs with strong mechanical properties and chemical compatibility with hydraulic fluids.

WEAR RINGS
- source states Mithson offers bearing/wear-ring materials with high wear resistance and friction performance
- function:
  - prevent metal-to-metal contact
  - guide piston
  - guide piston rod

PISTON SEALS
- dynamic seals
- single-acting or double-acting reciprocating motion
- prevent fluid from passing the piston
- act as a pressure barrier
- support lubrication film to reduce friction and wear

WIPER SEALS
- stop external contaminants entering the cylinder assembly
- contaminants are identified as a major cause of cylinder failure
- help return lubrication film into the cylinder during rod retraction

ROD SEALS
- identified as one of the most critical hydraulic-cylinder seals
- act as pressure barrier
- keep operating fluid inside cylinder
- prevent external leakage
- leakage can reduce equipment performance

MAIN STORY
- high-performance propulsion/transmission internals must withstand demanding conditions

- standard hardware is positioned as insufficient for those conditions
- Carbon-Filled PEEK thrust washers are presented as a polymer-engineering solution

WHY CHOOSE CARBON-FILLED PEEK?
- carbon-reinforced PEEK is positioned above virgin PEEK for the described duty
- high-modulus carbon fibers
- extreme axial-load management
- very low coefficient of friction

BENEFIT 1 — ADVANCED THERMAL MANAGEMENT
- high-temperature transmission environment
- carbon fibers improve thermal conductivity
- heat is dissipated away from friction interface
- reduces localized hot spots

- pressure × velocity is used as the relevant wear/operational criterion
- source claims the Carbon-Filled PEEK can handle nearly double the PV load of glass-filled alternatives
- applications:
  - Planetary Gear Carriers
  - Output Shaft Thrust Positions
  - High-Pressure Pump Wear Plates

BENEFIT 3 — SELF-LUBRICATING RELIABILITY
- carbon fiber acts as solid lubricant
- useful in boundary-lubrication events such as cold starts and high-torque shifts
- intended to prevent metal-to-metal galling
- intended to reduce parasitic power loss
