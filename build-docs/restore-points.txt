HOW TO USE THIS FILE
--------------------
Every piece of work below has a short name. Tell me the name and I can put
that part back. Two ways to go back:

  1. "Revert <name>"  — undoes only that one change and keeps everything
                        after it. This is almost always what you want.
  2. "Go back to <name>" — puts the whole site back to how it was at that
                        moment, losing everything after it.

Each line shows:   NAME   |  commit  |  what it changed
A "before-..." tag is the site just BEFORE that work.
An "after-..." tag is the site just AFTER it.
